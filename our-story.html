Year one

The story so far

and we're just getting started

Summer 2026

Doors open on Blue Vista Lane

Gradys opens in Midvale with a long marble bar, red stools, booths for the crew and a TV wherever you look. Open every day from 11 AM to 1 AM.

August 1, 2026

Grand opening

We threw a party with swag at the door and a house and techno set that ran late into the night.

Fall 2026

Football season, Gradys style

Meaty Monday arrives with Monday Night Football, and Burger Thursday brings $13 burgers and a new feature every week with TNF.

October 29, 2026

The new menu drops

New additions include soups and Ski Resort Chili in a bread bowl, entrées after 4 PM, jalapeño popper wontons and the Uncrust We Trust Sunday. We were too busy that day to make a feature burger, so every burger was $13 instead.

November 7, 2026

Up early for the Holy War

We opened at 10 AM sharp so you could grab a seat and a drink before kickoff. Brunch was hot, the beers were cold and the game was loud.

Right now

Holiday party books are open

We're booking 21+ holiday parties and gift cards are in stock. Plan yours before December gets away from you.
