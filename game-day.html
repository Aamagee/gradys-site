The Game Day Playbook

How to do game day right

get here before kickoff

  1. Show up early for the big ones

    Booths go first. For huge games we open the doors early. We opened at 10 AM for the Holy War.

  2. Bring your ID

    We're 21+ and we scan every ID at the door, every day. No exceptions.

  3. Order for the table

    Wings, nachos, pretzel dips and Gra-deez Nuts. Shareables travel well from one screen to the next.

  4. Want your game on? Ask

    Use the form to send us the matchup and we'll do our best to get it on a screen.

  5. Watching with a crowd?

    Ten or more of you? Plan a watch party and we'll set you up.
