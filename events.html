Good to know

Party FAQ

Is every guest required to be 21+?

Yes. Gradys is a bar, so every guest needs a valid photo ID, and we scan every ID at the door. Let your guests know ahead of time.

How do I book?

Use the party planner above. It writes a DM with your date, group size and details, and you send it to us on Instagram at @gradys_midvale. We'll reply with availability.

Can you put our game on?

Usually, yes. Tell us the matchup when you book and we'll do our best to get it on the screens near your group.

How big a group can you handle?

Anything from a big booth to a good chunk of the room. The bigger the group, the earlier you should reach out, especially in December.

Can we order off the full menu?

Yes. Shareables like wings, nachos, pretzel dips and the chorizo cheese ball are made for groups. Entrées are served after 4 PM, and brunch runs Saturday and Sunday from 11 to 3.

Any food allergies we should know about?

Let us know when you book. Our kitchen handles all 9 major allergens daily, including in our shared fryers, and the team will take the proper precautions.
